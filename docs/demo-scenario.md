# MATILDA Canonical Demo Scenario

## Purpose

This is the single end-to-end story used to design, seed, test, and demonstrate the prototype. All data is fictional and must be presented as synthetic.

## Demonstration objective

Show how one customer Observation changes Account understanding, affects the Account Plan, creates a decision in Account Review, may develop into a commercial Opportunity, and ultimately produces new learning.

The same information must move through the lifecycle. It must not be manually recreated in separate documents.

## Fictional Account

**Account:** Nordic Horizon Group  
**Profile:** A Nordic group with several business units and countries. It is exploring how to improve sharing of customer and product data across the organisation.  
**NOVA position:** Existing deliveries and relevant capabilities in parts of the group, but no group-wide relationship or defined Opportunity.

### Starting situation

- customer knowledge is distributed across several NOVA teams;
- relationships are concentrated around a few people;
- data fragmentation has previously been mentioned as a problem;
- NOVA has relevant expertise and deliveries in two parts of the group;
- there is no confirmed programme, budget, or Opportunity; and
- the decision structure is not understood.

## Actors

All names and roles used in seed data should be fictional.

- **Consultant** — captures the initial Observation.
- **Account Manager** — validates Account relevance and remains accountable.
- **Technical Account Manager** — connects capabilities and technical context.
- **Digital Platform lead at the customer** — possible initiative owner, initially unconfirmed.
- **Account Review participants** — inspect evidence and make the investment decision.
- **MATILDA** — classifies, connects, explains, and recommends without deciding.

## Triggering Observation

After a customer meeting, the consultant records:

> The customer appears to be considering a shared Nordic data platform to improve the sharing of customer and product data across business units. The initiative may be anchored in digital leadership, but scope and timing are not known.

Initial classification:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Information type | Observation |
| Topic | Data strategy |
| Importance | High |
| Status | Needs Validation |
| Source | Customer meeting |
| Confidence | Medium |
| Customer programme confirmed | No |
| Opportunity created | No |

## Existing context

MATILDA can connect the new Observation to three seeded items:

1. An earlier Observation that fragmented data slows cross-business digital services.
2. A Source describing Nordic Horizon Group's ambition for more integrated digital customer experiences.
3. A Relationship showing that NOVA knows technical stakeholders in two business units but lacks a group-level decision relationship.

These links should be visible as evidence, not silently merged into one claim.

## Stage 1 — Capture

### User action

The consultant enters the Observation through a short capture form or contextual assistant interaction.

### Experience requirement

Capture should take less than one minute. The consultant does not need to choose every field or understand the full domain model.

### MATILDA response

MATILDA proposes:

- Account: Nordic Horizon Group;
- topic: Data strategy;
- information type: Observation;
- importance: High;
- related Insights and Sources;
- candidate Knowledge Gaps;
- possibly affected Goal or Initiative; and
- next Actions.

### Human control

The consultant or Account Manager can accept, edit, or reject every material proposal.

### Demo message

> Information is captured once. MATILDA structures, connects, and proposes. The Account team validates.

## Stage 2 — Understand

### Knowledge Gaps created or proposed

- Who owns the initiative?
- Is this exploration or an approved programme?
- Which business units are involved?
- Is funding available or planned?
- What outcome is the customer seeking?
- What is the expected timing?
- Which existing partners are involved?

### Account-level interpretation

MATILDA may suggest:

> The topic appears strategically relevant because it aligns with an existing customer problem and NOVA has related capabilities. The initiative is not yet confirmed, and ownership, scope, funding, and timing remain unknown.

The suggestion must display its evidence and missing information.

### Demo message

> MATILDA shows both what the team knows and what it needs to learn next.

## Stage 3 — Account Planning

After human validation, the new Insight affects the generated Account Plan.

### Existing Goal

> Strengthen NOVA's strategic position in Nordic Horizon Group's digital development agenda.

### Proposed Initiative

> Develop NOVA's position around the customer's shared data platform direction.

### Proposed Actions

1. Validate the customer need with digital leadership.
2. Identify the initiative owner and decision structure.
3. Map affected business units and stakeholders.
4. Assemble relevant NOVA capabilities and references.
5. Prepare a point of view on a possible approach.
6. Explore whether the customer wants an initial workshop.

### Current position summary

| Area | Current state |
|---|---|
| Customer ambition | Better data sharing and integrated services |
| NOVA relevance | Relevant capabilities and existing delivery footprint |
| Position weakness | No confirmed group-level relationship |
| Key gaps | Owner, funding, scope, timing, buying process |
| Immediate next step | Validation discussion |

### Human control

The Account Manager can accept, edit, reject, or defer proposed changes and Actions.

### Demo message

> The Account Plan is not rewritten each quarter; it evolves as validated knowledge changes.

## Stage 4 — Account Review

Before the next Review, one validation conversation has taken place.

### New evidence

- the customer confirms the data fragmentation problem;
- a technical sponsor is identified;
- no funding has been allocated;
- the programme scope is still exploratory; and
- a global technology partner is already involved.

### Generated Review content

#### What changed

A possible group-wide data platform Initiative has been identified and the underlying problem has been confirmed.

#### What we learned

The customer is interested in exploring approaches, but scope, funding, and formal ownership remain unclear.

#### Progress

- customer problem validated: Completed;
- technical sponsor identified: Completed;
- decision owner mapped: Partial;
- relevant NOVA team assembled: Completed;
- exploratory workshop agreed: Not yet.

#### Risks

- an existing global technology partner may establish position early;
- NOVA's relationship remains mainly technical;
- investment could be premature without clearer sponsorship; and
- the Account team may confuse customer interest with a funded programme.

#### Decision required

> Should NOVA invest capacity in developing a clear perspective and proposing an exploratory workshop before a formal Opportunity exists?

### Decision options

- **Prioritise** — assemble a cross-functional team and prepare the workshop.
- **Observe** — continue validation without further material investment.
- **Stop** — do not pursue because strategic relevance or position is insufficient.

### Canonical decision for the demo

> The Account team approves preparing a focused exploratory workshop, conditional on validating the decision owner and customer process.

### Resulting Actions

- prepare a one-page workshop proposition;
- confirm customer participants and decision owner;
- identify a NOVA workshop lead; and
- agree a target date.

### Demo message

> Account Review becomes a place for prioritisation and decisions, not a manual review of stale information.

## Stage 5 — Opportunity development

After further customer dialogue, MATILDA can propose a potential Opportunity. It must not do so directly from the original Observation.

### Potential Opportunity

**Title:** Nordic Data Platform Advisory  
**Related Initiative:** Develop position around shared data platform  
**Customer need:** Improve data sharing and support more integrated digital services  
**NOVA proposition:** Exploratory advisory and architecture engagement

### Qualification view

| Dimension | State | Evidence or gap |
|---|---|---|
| Customer need confirmed | Confirmed | Customer validation meeting |
| Business importance understood | Confirmed | Strategy Source and meeting evidence |
| Sponsor identified | Confirmed | Technical sponsor named |
| Decision maker identified | Missing | Group-level owner not confirmed |
| Decision process understood | Missing | Customer process unknown |
| Funding path understood | Uncertain | No allocated budget |
| Timing understood | Uncertain | Possible next-half-year activity |
| NOVA relevance established | Confirmed | Relevant capability and delivery history |
| Competitive position understood | Partial | Existing global partner involved |
| Next customer commitment | Confirmed | Explore workshop format |

### MATILDA recommendation

> Do not classify the Opportunity as qualified yet. Customer need and NOVA relevance are supported, but the decision owner, funding path, and buying process remain unclear.

### Demo message

> MATILDA develops the team's understanding of whether the Opportunity is real; it does not merely move a record through stages.

## Stage 6 — Learning loop

An Action is completed: the team meets the customer sponsor.

### Action outcome

> The customer confirms the need, but says funding cannot be allocated until the next fiscal planning cycle. A formal decision is expected in approximately six months.

### Learning created

- **Fact:** Funding is not currently allocated.
- **Insight:** Timing is later than initially assumed.
- **Opportunity impact:** Timing and qualification change.
- **Account impact:** The Initiative remains relevant but the investment profile should be adjusted.
- **Knowledge Gap resolved:** Near-term budget status.
- **New Knowledge Gap:** What must happen in the next planning cycle for funding to be approved?

### Demo message

> Outcomes update both the Opportunity and the wider Account model. The loop closes through learning.

## Primary demo path

1. Open Account Overview.
2. Show the current strategic situation and important Knowledge Gaps.
3. Capture the new customer Observation.
4. Inspect MATILDA's proposed classification and links.
5. Review supporting Sources and missing information.
6. Accept or edit the proposed Observation and Knowledge Gaps.
7. Show the Account Plan impact.
8. Accept or edit a proposed Action.
9. Open the generated Account Review.
10. Show what changed since the previous Review.
11. Record the human Decision.
12. Show the Decision creating accountable Actions.
13. Open the potential Opportunity.
14. Show confirmed, uncertain, and missing qualification dimensions.
15. Add evidence from the sponsor meeting.
16. Show the qualification and timing changing.
17. Return to Customer Intelligence and show the new learning.

## Seed-data guidance

For the main demo Account, target coherent—not maximal—data:

- 25–50 Insights;
- 10–20 Sources;
- 5–10 Knowledge Gaps;
- 8–15 Relationships;
- 2–4 Goals;
- 3–6 Initiatives;
- 10–20 Actions;
- 2–5 Opportunities; and
- 2–4 Account Reviews.

Every seed record should support the same understandable story. Avoid random data added only to make screens look full.

## Demo guardrails

- Label all data as fictional/synthetic.
- Keep the original Observation separate from later confirmed Facts.
- Do not auto-create an Opportunity from the Observation.
- Show at least one rejected or edited AI proposal to demonstrate human control.
- Show evidence and uncertainty, not only conclusions.
- Keep the Account Review focused on a real decision.
- Ensure completed Actions can produce outcomes and new Insights.
- Make the primary path deterministic enough for a reliable demonstration.
